Privacy Policy

One day at a time. Every day counts.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

DayCounter is a simple Android app designed to help you track the number of days since your last alcoholic drink. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use DayCounter.

1. Information used by the app

DayCounter uses the information needed to display your alcohol-free day count and preferences. This may include your selected starting date, current count, app theme, and settings.

These details are intended to be stored locally on your device, rather than in an online account.

2. Personal information and accounts

DayCounter does not require you to create an account or enter your name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information to use its day-counting features.

3. How your information is used

The information stored by DayCounter is used to:

  • Calculate and display the number of days since your last alcoholic drink.
  • Keep your home screen widget up to date.
  • Remember your theme and other app preferences.
  • Allow you to reset or adjust your counter using the app's controls.

4. Sharing and third-party services

DayCounter is designed to work without a developer-operated account or cloud synchronisation service. Its day-counting features do not require your sobriety information to be sent to the developer.

The app may be distributed through Google Play, which processes information according to Google's own policies. This policy does not cover information collected independently by Google Play or the Android operating system.

5. Storage and deletion

Your counter data and settings are intended to remain on your device. You can change or reset your counter within the app. You can also remove locally stored app data through Android's app settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

6. Security

DayCounter aims to minimise the information it handles. Device-level protections, including your screen lock and Android security settings, help protect locally stored data. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

DayCounter is intended as a personal alcohol-free day tracker and is not specifically directed at children. The app does not require children or adults to provide identifying information to use its core features.

8. Health and medical advice

DayCounter is a day-counting tool, not a medical device, treatment service, or substitute for professional medical advice. If you are concerned about alcohol dependence or withdrawal, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DayCounter's functionality or information-handling practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.

10. Contact

For questions about DayCounter or this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at contact@glassops.co.uk.
